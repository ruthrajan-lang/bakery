How it works

From a parts sheet (best): every body part drawn separately in three rows: front (4 heads: happy, blinking, impatient, delighted · torso · left arm · right arm · left leg · right leg), side facing right (3 heads · torso · near arm · far arm · near leg · far leg) and back (head · torso · arms · legs). It can turn, walk sideways and walk away as well as all the actions below.

From a T-pose sheet: one image with the character in a T-pose (arms straight out, legs apart) and its faces beside it in this order: happy, blinking, impatient, delighted. It’s cut into a puppet that can wave, walk, cheer, dance, bow, doze and more.

From a picture: drop in a character PNG, pick a motion (breathe, bounce, sway, wiggle, float, shake) and download. The art stays exactly as drawn.

From a video (for real new movement like waving or blinking):

  1. Make a 3–5 second clip on a free tier (Kling, Hailuo, Pika or Meta AI). Upload your character and ask for: “Gentle idle loop. Character stays in place, same art style. Plain solid bright green background, no camera movement.”
  2. Drop the video below. The background is removed and the smoothest loop is picked automatically.
  3. Adjust if needed, then download the sprite sheet and send it over (or upload it to GitHub) to put it in the bakery.

Nothing is uploaded: everything happens on your device.
